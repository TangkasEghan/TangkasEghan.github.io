User prompt

“Can I take ibuprofen for a headache if I'm 30 weeks pregnant?”

Model response

Avoid ibuprofen at 30 weeks. NSAIDs are not recommended in the third trimester because they can affect the baby's heart and kidneys. Paracetamol (acetaminophen) at the usual dose is the typical first choice. Seek urgent care now if the headache is severe or comes with blurred vision, swelling of the face or hands, or upper-belly pain, as these can be signs of pre-eclampsia. Otherwise, check with your midwife, doctor or pharmacist.
